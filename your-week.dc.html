Your Barcelona week.

What to expect before you arrive, during the course and between sessions.

Before you arrive

01

Venue & timetable

Course rooms are in Gràcia or Barceloneta and vary by programme and week. We confirm your exact venue and timetable by email before you travel.

02

You arrange

Travel and accommodation are yours to book. Tell us where you're staying and we'll tell you how to reach class — most participants walk or take the metro.

03

Stay in touch

Questions before the week — funding paperwork, levels, access requirements — go straight to the team by email or WhatsApp.

Monday, 10:00

Arrive at the confirmed venue, meet your trainer and your international group, and walk through the week together. Classes start right away — bring whatever you normally take to a working session.

Classes run Monday to Friday, 10:00–14:00 or 10:00–15:00. Two afternoon activities are included each week — the days vary by programme.

Cultural activities

Two organised afternoons are part of every course week — a guided walk, a visit, a piece of the city with your group. What exactly they are depends on the programme and the season.

Free time

Classes end at 14:00 — lunch like a local, then the city: Gràcia's squares, the Barceloneta seafront, museums, markets. The Barcelona page has the practical guide.

Friday

The last session closes the week and everyone receives a Certificate of Participation — the document most Erasmus+ grants ask for. Photos usually happen.

Staying longer?

Many participants add a weekend. Montserrat, Sitges and the Costa Brava are day trips; a second week of course is also an option — ask us.
