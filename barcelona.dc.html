Barcelona,
while you're here.

The practical guide for course participants — venues, neighbourhoods, getting around, and what to do when class ends.

BARCELONETA

Where courses happen

Two areas: Gràcia, where the SpainBcn headquarters are (Carrer del Pare Lainez 19), and Barceloneta, a few streets from the sea. The exact room depends on your programme and week — always confirmed by email before you travel.

Where to stay

Anywhere near a metro line works. Gràcia and Eixample are close to the headquarters; El Born and Barceloneta suit the seaside rooms. We don't sell accommodation — tell us your neighbourhood and we'll tell you the commute.

Getting to class

Both areas are on the metro and well served by buses. Most participants walk or ride 15–20 minutes from their accommodation. A T-casual ticket covers the week comfortably.

From the airport

Aerobús to Plaça de Catalunya, the L9 metro, or a taxi — expect 25–45 minutes into town depending on traffic and where you're staying.

Lunch & rhythm

Class ends at 14:00 — exactly when Barcelona sits down to eat. Look for the menú del día: a fixed-price lunch most restaurants serve on weekdays. Dinner here starts late, rarely before 21:00.

Free afternoons

A short honest list: the beach at Barceloneta, Parc de la Ciutadella, the Gothic Quarter and El Born, Gaudí (book Sagrada Família and Park Güell ahead), and Gràcia's squares at dusk. One per afternoon is plenty.

Languages

You'll hear Catalan and Spanish side by side — both are Barcelona's languages. Courses run in English or Spanish, and you'll get by fine with either.

Accessibility

If you have access requirements — mobility, hearing, anything — tell us before you travel so we can plan the right room and route.

Barcelona by season

SPRING

Mild and busy with course weeks. Terraces open, city walks at their best.

SUMMER

Hot, bright, beach after class. The busiest season — book accommodation early.

AUTUMN

Warm well into October. The city back at work — a good time for school groups.

WINTER

Quiet and clear. Museums without queues, the city to yourself.
