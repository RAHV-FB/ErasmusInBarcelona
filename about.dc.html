Erasmus in Barcelona is SpainBcn in Barcelona.

This website is the Barcelona-focused home of SpainBcn-Programs — the place to choose, organise and understand an Erasmus+ mobility specifically in Barcelona.

SpainBcn-Programs was established in Barcelona in 1997 and also runs programmes in Málaga, Mallorca, Gran Canaria, Tenerife and Tarragona — all on SpainBcn.com ↗.

Since 1997

SpainBcn-Programs was founded in 1997 by María Angeles and Miriam to share their city and culture with students from around the world. It has since trained thousands of teachers, professors, staff and students from all over Europe through Erasmus+ KA1 mobility.

Today the programme runs around twenty courses across some thirty weeks a year, taught in two Barcelona neighbourhoods: Gràcia, where the headquarters are, and Barceloneta by the sea.

The team

Every trainer is a native speaker with specialised teaching certifications. Miriam coordinates the programmes; Adriana keeps the logistics running.
